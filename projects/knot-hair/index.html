Styling hair as a way to arrange sound.

Styling hair as a way to arrange sound.

Wired earphones become the hair on a model head. Binaural microphones let the person styling it listen from the model's ears. Moving a strand changes where its sound comes from and how it overlaps with the others. A hairstyle and a way of hearing it take shape together.
